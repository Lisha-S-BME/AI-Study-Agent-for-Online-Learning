# AI Study Agent for Online Learning

An AI-based study monitoring and reminder system designed to solve low engagement, distraction, and inconsistent learning habits faced by online learners.

## Problem Statement
Many students enrolled in online courses struggle to stay consistent due to inactivity, social media distractions, lack of motivation, and absence of personalized guidance. This can lead to poor engagement, incomplete courses, and reduced learning outcomes.

## Solution
This AI Study Agent monitors student study activity in real time through a rule-based intelligent system. It detects inactivity, identifies distractions, tracks weak learning areas, and provides personalized reminders, motivational alerts, and study suggestions. The system is simple, feasible, and can be integrated into online learning platforms to improve consistency, engagement, and course completion rates.

## Features
- **Study Activity Tracking** – Monitors study progress module-wise to understand learning patterns.
- **Inactivity Detection** – Detects when a student is idle for too long and triggers reminders.
- **Distraction Monitoring** – Identifies distractions such as social media usage.
- **Weak Area Identification** – Analyzes subjects/topics needing more focus.
- **Personalized Reminders** – Sends motivational and adaptive reminders to resume studying.
- **Study Suggestions** – Recommends topics to revise based on weak areas.
- **Engagement Score Calculation** – Measures student engagement level numerically.
- **Automated Study Schedule** – Generates a structured study plan for better time management.

## Tools & Technologies Used
- **Python** – Core programming language used to build the system logic.
- **Google Colab** – Cloud-based environment used for development and execution.
- **PyTorch** – Used for engagement score calculation and AI-based extensions.
- **Random / Time / Collections** – Used for simulation, timing, and activity tracking.
- **Rule-Based AI Logic** – Used for decision-making and personalized recommendations.

## Benefits / Impact
This system can help:
- Improve student focus and consistency in online learning.
- Reduce distractions and procrastination.
- Increase course completion rates.
- Provide personalized support without human intervention.
- Enhance learning outcomes through timely reminders and suggestions.
